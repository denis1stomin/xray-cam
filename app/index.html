X-Ray Cam

Point your phone's camera at your hand and see the bones move.

Just for fun. It's a toy, not a medical device: the bones are drawn by the app.
The video stays on your phone and is never uploaded.

How it works

The camera picture is turned into a negative with an X-ray glow. When your hand is in view, hand tracking finds its 21 joints and the app draws a skeleton that follows them.

The bones are an illustration, not your real bones. Please don't use this for anything medical.

Everything runs on your phone. Nothing is uploaded or stored, except the pictures you choose to save.

Hand tracking: Google MediaPipe (Apache 2.0).
